# Hi, I'm Fernanda Cortés 👋

**Economist (MA) → Data Scientist · Causal Inference · Experimentation**
Based in Mexico City.

I turn dispersed, person-dependent information into ordered, replicable systems —
and I translate rigorous analysis into something a business decision-maker can
use. When the data to answer a question doesn't exist yet, **I build it** (web
scraping, NLP, integrating official sources).

---

### What I do

- **Causal inference & experimentation** — difference-in-differences, event
  study, IV, RDD; panel data; A/B test design. My master's thesis uses a
  quasi-experimental design on close elections (jury noted **potential for
  publication**).
- **Data from scratch** — large-scale scraping + NLP over unstructured text,
  including **GenAI (LLM) extraction of structured variables** from news, geolocated
  to build indicators that official data doesn't capture.
- **The bridge from rigor to decisions** — I sit between the people who guarantee
  statistical rigor and the business readers who need to act, and I make the two
  understand each other.
- **Systems, not one-offs** — self-updating pipelines, documented and modular, so
  the work is reproducible and nobody is a single point of failure.
